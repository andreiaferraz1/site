/ The Challenge
02
How do you make an online language school feel as personal, trusted and human as a classroom?
The brand needed to translate warmth and rigor into a fully-digital experience — across student touchpoints, marketing and the learning product itself. Every element had to scale: from social posts to ebooks, from classroom decks to the website.
