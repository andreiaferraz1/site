Quantum Deck

Hard science,
told like a story worth funding.
/ Overview
01
A technical quantum computing presentation, rebuilt as an investor-ready narrative that holds up in front of physicists and partners alike.
/ The challenge
02
The original deck was accurate and unreadable: dense equations, screenshot charts and paragraphs of body copy competing on every slide. The redesign kept every technical claim intact but rebuilt the hierarchy around it — one idea per slide, schematics drawn rather than pasted, and a consistent dark-to-light rhythm that separates the science section from the business case. The result reads as a single argument: quantum advantage is near, and this is the team positioned to capture it.
