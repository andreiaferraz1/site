About

WHERE I CAME FROM
Born and raised in Brazil, I grew up in a world of diversity, creativity, and constant change—an experience that has shaped me into a versatile and resilient designer.
/02
THE BEGINNING
I began my journey at as an intern and eventually rose to the position of Senior Brand Designer. In this role, I managed across 11 units, 500+ collaborators, overseeing offline/digital marketing with a $300K annual budget. / AI
05

Designing at the edge
of what's possible

AI is reshaping creative work. I'm here to explore it thoughtfully and intentionally.
Design has always been about curiosity, about asking better questions, seeing new connections, and finding unexpected directions. As a Senior Brand Designer, I see AI not as a replacement for creative thinking, but as a new kind of collaborator that can expand the early, messy, exploratory parts of the process.

I'm not an "AI expert" (yet) but I'm a designer who stays open to new tools, experiments constantly, and brings critical judgment to every output. My role is to make sure that technology serves the brand, the strategy, and the story, not the other way around.
Curiosity as a practice
I've built my career on staying adaptable. The tools change; the need for clear ideas, strong narratives, and human-centered design doesn't. AI is the latest shift, and I'm approaching it the same way: learn by doing, stay skeptical of hype, and only adopt what actually improves the work.
How AI fits into my workflow
I use AI to move faster in the places where speed matters most—early exploration, concept generation, and iteration. This frees me to spend more time on the strategic and craft decisions that define a great brand: positioning, storytelling, visual systems, and the details that make an experience feel human.
What stays human
AI can generate options, but it can't replace taste, context, or creative direction. Strategy, narrative, and judgment remain central to my process. Every AI output is a starting point, not a finished idea. My job is to shape, refine, and align it with real business goals and human needs.

My AI-Powered Creative Workflow

ChatGPT
I use it as a strategic brainstorming partner during the early stages of a project. It helps me expand ideas, challenge assumptions, and explore different perspectives before moving into design, creating a broader creative space where I can identify stronger ideas and refine them through my own design expertise.

‍Example:
Exploring multiple brand narrative directions for a healthcare campaign, then testing which message feels more human, relevant, and aligned with the audience.
/01
Claude
I recently started experimenting with Claude and have been incorporating it more into my creative workflow, especially for deeper analysis, research synthesis, and organizing complex information.

Its ability to process large amounts of content makes it a valuable partner when working through stakeholder feedback, brand documentation, and creative strategy. I use it to synthesize conversations, structure brand principles, explore messaging directions, and identify opportunities to create more consistent brand experiences.

‍Example:
Turning stakeholder interviews and project notes into a clear set of brand principles that help guide visual direction, messaging, and campaign development.
/02
Adobe Firefly
Adobe Firefly is part of my visual exploration process. I use it to quickly test creative territories, explore different moods, and build visual references before investing time in final production.It helps me experiment with imagery styles, textures, compositions, lighting approaches, and campaign directions — allowing me to move from abstract ideas to more tangible concepts faster.

‍Example:
Creating early visual explorations for a healthcare campaign to define the emotional tone and photography direction before developing final creative assets.
/03
Figma AI
Figma is where ideas become structured design solutions. With AI features integrated into the workflow, I use it to speed up repetitive tasks, explore layout options, and communicate ideas more efficiently.AI helps me generate starting points, but the design decisions — hierarchy, storytelling, accessibility, visual language, and brand consistency — remain driven by my experience as a designer.

‍Example:
Using AI-assisted layout exploration to quickly test landing page structures, then refining the experience through brand strategy, design systems, and user-focused decisions.
