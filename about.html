N. 03
About
OVERALL
For over 15 years, I've dedicated my career to transforming complex ideas into powerful brand stories that captivate and inspire. With a deep passion for design, strategy, and collaboration, I’ve had the privilege of working with global teams to create cohesive, engaging brand experiences that deliver results.
/01
WHERE I CAME FROM
Born and raised in Brazil, I grew up in a world of diversity, creativity, and constant change—an experience that has shaped me into a versatile and resilient designer.
/02
THE BEGINNING
I began my journey at as an intern and eventually rose to the position of Senior Brand Designer. In this role, I managed across 11 units, 500+ collaborators, overseeing offline/digital marketing with a $300K annual budget. My focus was on repositioning the Chromos school’s brand, which enhanced its relevance in the competitive education market in Southeastern Brazil — this experience was pivotal in shaping my career and reinforced my belief in the transformative power of design.
/03
EXPANDED MY HORIZONS
In 2015, I expanded my horizons, joining Agência 3Nos as Art Director. Working with world-class businesses, I led +100 projects spanning industries from education to technology, automotive, healthcare, and e-commerce—across USA, Canada, Brazil, and Uruguay. I saw firsthand how strategic design could influence markets and build brands that resonate with diverse audiences.
/04
PROUND OF
I worked on projects at Babco Design Services, where I collaborated closely with my manager, a former Airbnb designer, to design and evolve scalable brand systems. This role allowed me to work with cross-functional teams at top companies like Amazon, Instagram, Samsung, Airbnb, Google, and Meta, as well as Silicon Valley tech companies, including quantum computing and AI startups, ensuring alignment with strategic goals.Now, I’m seeking a long-term opportunity to apply my expertise in strategic design, creativity, and collaboration to build impactful brands that resonate and drive results.
/05
NOW
I stay current with design trends in both B2B and B2C tech industries, ensuring innovative and impactful creative solutions. My work spans from Silicon Valley tech companies to global healthcare systems. Effective communication and teamwork define my approach, with proven success in fast-paced startups, B2B industries, and remote work environments.
/06
FUTURE
I’m seeking a long-term opportunity to apply my expertise in strategic design, creativity, and collaboration to build impactful brands that resonate and drive results.
