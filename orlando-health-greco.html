/ The Challenge
N. 02
Transforming a transit station into an immersive healthcare brand experience
The objective was to create a cohesive environmental campaign that extended the "One For All." brand platform across every architectural touchpoint—from station entrances and structural pillars to banners, building headers, and wayfinding elements—reinforcing FMCOH as the region's destination for complete, connected healthcare.

Rather than relying on traditional advertising placements, the project transformed the station itself into a branded environment where every commuter interaction became an opportunity to strengthen awareness, communicate key service offerings, and drive appointment scheduling.

Also Greco Station presented a highly complex environment with numerous architectural elements, each requiring unique production specifications:
01
Maintained a consistent visual language across 60+ placements.
02
Adapt messaging without feeling repetitive.
03
Balance brand awareness with service education.
04
Designed for multiple viewing distances and commuter perspectives.
05
Meet strict transit authority (HART) branding approval requirements.
06
Adapted the system across diverse architectural elements.

/ CONCEPT & Applications
N. 04
Creative Strategy bringing the “Complete Connected Care” vision to life through thoughtful, connected design.
The messaging intentionally avoided clinical terminology and statistics, focusing instead on clear, memorable statements that communicated the brand’s core values: complete care, connected healthcare, community wellness, convenience, trusted expertise, and accessibility.

Visually, photography emphasized healthy lifestyles, authentic human moments, and meaningful connections, supported by bold typography and clean compositions designed to remain highly legible within a dynamic transit environment.

/ Alternative Concept
N. 05
Alternative brand direction: A softer expression of the Orlando Health brand
This concept presents an alternative interpretation of the Orlando Health brand, evolving its visual identity with a softer, cleaner, and more modern aesthetic while preserving its core brand principles.

Instead of relying on high-energy and boldness visuals, this approach embraces a calmer, more approachable tone, resulting in a brand experience that feels clean, welcoming, and reassuring while preserving a strong connection to the Orlando Health brand.
