/ The Challenge
02
A user-friendly system empowering Apolitical team to create cohesive, on-brand social media content at scale.
Apolitical needed a scalable design system that would empower their marketing team—including members without design experience—to create cohesive and visually consistent social media posts, primarily for LinkedIn. The challenge was to craft visuals that not only fit within the client's existing brand guidelines but also told a compelling and engaging story across various touchpoints. By focusing on clear and impactful visual elements, this approach translated complex messages into easy-to-understand visuals, enhancing client engagement.

/ Desirable STEPS
06
Design doesn’t end at launch — it’s about measuring impact, learning from data, and iterating strategically.
Establish
Performance
Context
Every visual identity should drive brand perception, recognition, and engagement. Monitoring how the refreshed LinkedIn identity is applied across platforms ensures consistency and impact.
KPIs
Success is measured through brand recognition, engagement, user retention, and sentiment from users and internal teams. Dashboards and reports make these insights actionable.
Feedbacks
Regular reviews with cross-functional teams and user testing inform iterative improvements in color, typography, iconography, and layout, ensuring the system evolves effectively.
Iteration
The identity is a living system, updated for new campaigns, global markets, and device performance, with documented changes showing measurable progress.
Strategic Priorities
The redesign maintains professional credibility, supports global scalability, optimizes for digital experiences, and ensures flexibility for future growth.
