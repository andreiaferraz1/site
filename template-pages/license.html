/ Desirable  STEPS
02
Design doesn’t end at launch — it’s about measuring impact, learning from data, and iterating strategically.
Establish Performance
Context Every visual identity should drive brand perception, recognition, and engagement. Monitoring how the refreshed LinkedIn identity is applied across platforms ensures consistency and impact.
KPIs Success is measured through brand recognition, engagement, user retention, and sentiment from users and internal teams. Dashboards and reports make these insights actionable.
Feedbacks Regular reviews with cross-functional teams and user testing inform iterative improvements in color, typography, iconography, and layout, ensuring the system evolves effectively.
Iteration The identity is a living system, updated for new campaigns, global markets, and device performance, with documented changes showing measurable progress.
Strategic Priorities The redesign maintains professional credibility, supports global scalability, optimizes for digital experiences, and ensures flexibility for future growth.
