Brandmarch Social Media

Commercial real estate expertise, market activity and local insights transformed into clear, compelling visual stories.
/ Overview
N. 01

Connecting brands with the people, places and insights shaping high streets.

Brandmarch is built around local commercial real estate expertise. Its social content spans broker profiles, street insights, store openings, shopping centers and development alerts.

I developed a modular visual system that organizes this range into recognizable content families while preserving enough flexibility for every story to feel distinct.

/ Design Process
N. 03

Building the system through exploration and refinement.

Four rounds traced the relationship between portraits, places, information, brand elements and editorial composition.

Content architecture
Defined the hierarchy and purpose for people, places, insights and updates.
Visual exploration
Tested scale, image treatment, typography and the balance between editorial and graphic elements.
System development
Built repeatable structures for profiles, streets, stores, centers and alerts.
Refinement
Consolidated the strongest ideas, improving legibility, pacing and consistency.
/ The Design System
N. 04

One visual language. Multiple content stories.

The framework creates consistency through typography, color, image behavior, graphic devices and carousel pacing — not a single repeated template.

Local Expert Profiles
Portrait-led stories that connect brokers, markets and locations.
Street Profiles
High streets told through places, openings and market facts.
Brand Store Features
Architecture, location context and retail perspectives.
Shopping Center Features
Destination imagery, project facts and leasing opportunities.
Featured Alerts
Timely stories around development and market activity.
Brandmarch Stories
The platform, its founders and a community of expertise.
Color
Deep charcoal and white create authority. Restrained green provides identification.
Typography
Bold headlines establish hierarchy, supported by a clear reading scale.
Imagery
Portraits and architectural scenes bring the people and places into focus.
Graphic elements
Linear motifs, cropped images and consistent spacing connect the system.
/ System in Use
N. 05

Built for the story. Connected by design.

A family of social formats brings local expertise and market stories together. Portraits introduce the people; image-led compositions establish the place; structured cards make the detail easy to navigate.
